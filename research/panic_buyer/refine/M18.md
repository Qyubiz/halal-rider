## 1. Is 90% mode itself luck? (stationary block bootstrap of its monthly returns, periods it was not picked on)
| Period | Months | Compounded %/month | 90% interval | Chance true rate <= 0 | <= 3%/month | Months > 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-26 | 34 | +6.8 | +4.4 to +9.4 | 0.0% | 0.5% | 74% |
| 2018-20 | 36 | +10.8 | +3.9 to +18.5 | 0.4% | 3.1% | 78% |
The edge is real in both untouched periods; its size is uncertain (roughly 4-9%/month on recent data).

## 2. Holding BTC while 90% mode waits (upper bound: dips that fill while the cash is in BTC still count as bought)
Compounded %/month / worst drop. 90% mode: 2021-23 +13.17 / -64, 2024-26 +6.89 / -35, 2018-20 +10.82 / -66.
Best variant (BTC only while above its 200-day average and no coin within 3% of its trigger):
+13.81 / -65, +6.06 / -47, +12.13 / -71. Every variant lowers 2024-26 profit and deepens the drop, even as an
upper bound: BTC falls in the hours before the dips the bot wants to buy. Not worth building.
A first run of this test looked better (+7.6 in 2024-26) because it only held BTC in hours that turned out to have
no fill, a look-ahead error; fixed before reporting.
