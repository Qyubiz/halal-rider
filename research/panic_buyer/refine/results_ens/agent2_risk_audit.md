# Agent 2: Execution and risk audit of Panic Buyer (read-only)

**Scope:** `/home/user/halal-rider/panic_buyer.py` (490 lines), `/home/user/halal-rider/binance_spot.py` (133 lines), `deploy/panic-buyer*`, and how the bot interacts with `rider.py` on the same account. I did not edit or commit any repo file.

**How the findings were checked:** I wrote a reproduction script, `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-halal-rider/ec562e35-ea9e-5a74-ab92-4ff22c5eeb77/scratchpad/risk_repro.py`. It runs the unmodified `PanicBuyer` against the repo's own `tests/fake_binance.py`, using the real `panic_config.90wr.example.json` and `panic_config.10pct.example.json`. Each cycle is wrapped the same way `main()` does it: errors are logged and the loop continues. Results are in the "Repro" column and the table below. The backtest figures are quoted from `research/panic_buyer/refine/REFINE.md` and `HANDOFF.md`.

## Reproduced results (fake exchange, starting with 1,000 USDT)

| ID | Scenario | Mode | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Bot buys the 14% dip; the coin keeps falling to −70% within the hold window | 90% | Account 1000.00 → **310.00**. One trade, realized −690.00. The only exit was the time limit. |
| B | `GET /account` times out right after the buy fills | 90% | No position recorded, but **11.3839 coins held**, with no take-profit and no time limit. 100 h later the account is worth 816.84 and the coins are still untracked. |
| C | `POST /order` times out, but Binance accepted the order | 90% | Same as B: 11.3839 untracked coins. The bot also places a second buy with the cash it still has. |
| D | LUNA-style collapse to below the 5 USDT minimum order | 90% | Account value **20.05**, yet `trades=0`, `realized=0.00`, and `budget()` still reports (1000.0, 1000.0). The loss is never recorded. |
| E | Take-profit half filled, then the time-limit close fails on a 503 | 10% | The bot thinks it holds 11.3839 but really owns 5.6920. Next hour it sells 11.38, and the other bot's holding goes **10.0000 → 4.3081**. |
| F | Symbol halted (status BREAK) while the bot holds it | 10% | Still holding after 120 h. 74 rejected sell attempts and 73 identical hourly ALERT lines, with no escalation. |
| G | Three all-in losing trades in a row | 90% | 3 trades, 0 wins, realized −653.52, account **346.47**. The bot re-enters at 100% every time. |

## Failure points, ranked by severity

| # | Where | Failure scenario | Severity | Guardrail that fixes it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `panic_buyer.py:210-222` (`place_tp`), `281-308` (`manage_positions`), `301` (time limit) | **There is no price stop at all.** A position can only exit through the take-profit limit or the 48 h / 96 h time limit. In the all-in modes (`n_orders:1`, `max_order_pct:100`), a collapse after entry falls fully on the account. Repro A: −69%. Backtest: worst single trades −54% of the account (DOGE, Jan 2021) and −43% (RENDER, Nov 2022) (`REFINE.md:90`); all-in on one coin with LUNA/FTT/SRM added loses 99–100% in May 2022 (`REFINE.md:113`). | **Critical** | Replace the plain take-profit with a Binance spot OCO order: the take-profit limit plus a resting STOP_LOSS / STOP_LOSS_LIMIT leg that lives on the exchange, so it works even if the bot is down. Fall back to a stop the bot checks itself if the symbol does not allow OCO. A stop changes the win rate and return, so it must be re-backtested (`refine/engine.py`) before choosing a level. |
| 2 | `panic_buyer.py:171-177` (`budget`), `310-320` (`place_orders`); `panic_config.*.example.json` | **The whole account goes into one coin, and nothing protects the account as a whole.** `per = min(1.0*total, cash/1)`. There is no daily or monthly drawdown halt, no limit on consecutive losses (the only cooldown is per coin, `record()` line 127: 3 h in 90% mode), and no limit on risk per trade. Repro G: −65% over three losses with re-entry every time. `rider.py:304` has a weekly halt; Panic Buyer has nothing comparable. | **Critical** | **Max drawdown kill-switch:** before each cycle, mark the account to market (USDT plus held coins at bid). If it is down more than X% from the day's start or Y% from its rolling 30-day high: cancel buys, flatten all positions, and save `locked_until` so the lock survives restarts. **Consecutive-loss cooldown:** after N losing trades, no entries for T hours. **Per-trade risk cap:** reject the order if qty × (entry − hard stop) > Z% of equity. As written, every order fails this cap, because no stop exists (#1). |
| 3 | `panic_buyer.py:273-277` with `198-202`; `328-333` | **The buy fills, then `open_position` raises** (because `balances()` at line 200 fails). The order was already removed from state and saved at lines 274-275, so the filled coins are orphaned: no take-profit, no time limit, not counted in `budget()`. Repro B: 11.3839 coins orphaned. API errors are most likely during a crash, which is exactly when this path runs. | **High** | Write the position to state, using `executedQty` from the order, **before** any further API call. Fetch balances inside a try block and fall back to the fill quantity. At startup and every hour, reconcile state against `GET /api/v3/openOrders` and the account's own `pb-` clientOrderIds / `myTrades`, and alert on any balance not linked to a recorded position. |
| 4 | `binance_spot.py:59` (`attempts = 1` for POST), `76-77`; `panic_buyer.py:339-345`, `245-248` | **An order request times out or gets a 5xx, but Binance did execute it.** A buy ends up live or filled without the bot knowing (repro C). A time-limit market sell that went through is treated as failed, so next hour the bot sells `p["qty"]` again. That either fails forever or sells another bot's coins (same mechanism as E). The 15 s timeout (`binance_spot.py:30`) is typical of exchange stress. | **High** | Generate the `newClientOrderId` first and save it to state as *pending*. On any POST error without a definite 4xx rejection, query `GET /api/v3/order?origClientOrderId=` before doing anything else. Never resend a sell without checking what is actually free. |
| 5 | `panic_buyer.py:228-248` (`close_position`) | **The take-profit is cancelled and a partial fill read (`sold_q`), then `prices()` (line 243) or the market sell (line 245) raises.** `sold_q` is lost, `p["qty"]` is not reduced, and the partial P&L is not recorded. Next hour the bot sells its full original quantity and takes the other bot's coins. Repro E: halal-rider's 10.0000 coins became 4.3081. | **High** | Save `p["qty"] -= sold_q` and record the partial fill **immediately** after reading the cancelled take-profit. Cap every sell at `min(p["qty"], free balance not reserved by other bots)`. Keep the bot's coins in a separate Binance sub-account so the bots are actually isolated. |
| 6 | `panic_buyer.py:184` (status checked for entries only), `245`, `477-480` (exchange info refreshed only every 6 h) | **Exchange halt or delisting while the bot holds the coin.** FTT was halted from 15 Nov 2022 to 22 Sep 2023 and reopened 32% lower (`REFINE.md:38`). The time limit cannot sell. Each hour the market sell is rejected and the same alert repeats (repro F: 74 attempts, 73 identical alerts). Entries can use status data up to 6 h old. There is no delisting-schedule check (`HANDOFF.md:136,149`). With all the money in one coin, the whole account would be frozen. | **High** (1-coin) / Medium (8-coin) | Refresh `exchangeInfo` status every cycle. Poll `GET /sapi/v1/spot/delist-schedule` and the monitoring tag, then block entries and exit early on any coin that is listed. If a held coin is not TRADING, send one escalated alert and mark it `frozen` instead of retrying silently. Cap the share of capital in any single coin. |
| 7 | `panic_buyer.py:244-256` (also `213-215`) | **Collapse below the minimum order size.** If the remaining coins are worth less than 5 USDT, the position is deleted with `sold_q=0`, and only the cooldown is set. The loss is never recorded: `realized_usdt`, `trades` and `wins` stay unchanged and `budget()` still reports full capital. Repro D: the account is worth 20.05 while the bot reports 0 loss. A wipe-out never shows up in the live win rate, so any kill-switch based on realized P&L would never fire. | **High** | Always call `record()` with the mark price (value the leftover coins at bid) when closing below the minimum. Base the kill-switch on account equity, not realized P&L. |
| 8 | `panic_buyer.py:327-333` | **Market BUY sized with `quoteOrderQty`** whenever the price is already under the trigger: the whole account (in 1-coin mode) goes in with no price protection, no spread check and no depth check, into a coin that is falling fast. The decision uses `/ticker/price` (last trade, line 181), fetched before 19 sequential klines calls, so it can be several seconds old. | **High** (1-coin) | Use a LIMIT IOC order at `trigger × (1 + small cap)` instead of MARKET. Before entering, read `GET /api/v3/ticker/bookTicker` and block the entry if the spread is above its normal level or the order book is too thin for the order size. |
| 9 | `panic_buyer.py:242-248` | **Time-limit / panic sell is a MARKET order for the whole position**, with no check on slippage or order-book depth. In a gap or thin book, the fill price has no floor. | Medium | Exit with sliced LIMIT IOC orders at a bounded discount to the best bid, and escalate if they don't fill. Log the slippage on every fill. |
| 10 | `panic_buyer.py:486` (sleeps 1 h); `deploy/panic-buyer:10` | **The bot only looks once an hour.** A resting buy that fills mid-hour is not seen for up to 1 h, and an intra-hour crash after the fill is not seen until the next cycle (with no stop, nothing happens then either). `panic-buyer stop` leaves held coins **with no time limit** ("no 48h time limit while stopped"). A crash loop or outage means no position is managed. | Medium | Rely on stops that rest on the exchange (#1). Add a light sub-minute watchdog that only checks positions. Make `stop` either flatten positions or keep the watchdog running. |
| 11 | `panic_buyer.py:186-191` (crash guard) | **The crash guard only applies at entry** and looks only at the last closed hourly candle versus the 7-day high. It does not see a crash within the current hour, and it does nothing after entry. The research shows it is the only thing standing between all-in mode and a LUNA wipe-out (`REFINE.md:63,89`). | Medium | Keep the guard, but also block entries when BTC or the coin's 1 h ATR exceeds k × its rolling median, which is the tail/volatility filter requested. |
| 12 | `panic_buyer.py:179-195`; `binance_spot.py:30, 59-79` | **API slowness and stale data are never measured.** Each GET can take up to 3 tries × 15 s plus backoff. Nothing checks that the newest kline is the one just closed, or how long each request took, so triggers can be built from stale data during exchange stress. | Medium | Time every request. Block entries if any call in the cycle took more than N ms, or if `ks[-1].close_ms < t − 1 h`, or if the ticker and the last close diverge too far. |
| 13 | `panic_buyer.py:155-163` | **Binance error `-2013` (order does not exist) is treated as "cancelled, nothing filled".** If that response is wrong for an order that actually filled, a filled buy becomes orphaned coins, or a filled take-profit gets re-placed and the time-limit close then over-sells (same as #5). | Medium | On `-2013`, check `myTrades` for that orderId before assuming nothing filled. |
| 14 | `panic_buyer.py:198-202, 318-319`; `rider.py:150-158, 196-200` | **Both bots share the account and trade the same 19 coins.** Panic Buyer's `min(qty, free)` and the sells in #4/#5 can take halal-rider's free coins. Halal-rider's `equity()` counts all USDT, including Panic Buyer's. Neither bot's halt sees the other's exposure. | Medium | Use separate Binance sub-accounts, or a shared ledger file listing the coins each bot owns, plus an account-level kill-switch. |
| 15 | `panic_buyer.py:97-101` (`load_state`), `339-343`, `472-481` | **State and restarts.** A process crash between `new_order` and `save()` leaves a live order the bot does not know about. A corrupt `panic_state.json` causes a crash loop (`Restart=always`, 20 s), and while it loops no position is managed. Nothing reconciles state at startup. | Medium | Write pending intent to state before sending orders. Reconcile at startup (#3). Wrap state loading in a try block, keep a backup state file, and `fsync` before `os.replace`. |
| 16 | `binance_spot.py:44-45, 83-86`; `panic_buyer.py:477-480` | **Clock drift.** The clock offset is re-synced only every 6 h with `recvWindow=5000`. More than 5 s of drift makes every signed call fail with `-1021`, including exits. | Low | Re-sync on any `-1021` and retry once; alert if the offset exceeds 1 s. |
| 17 | `panic_buyer.py:403-408` | **Alerts are sent synchronously** with a 10 s timeout and no rate limit. An outage produces a storm of identical alerts (repro F: 73), which hides new problems. | Low | Deduplicate alerts and escalate on state changes only. |
| 18 | `panic_buyer.py:174` | **Budget is valued at entry cost, not market value.** With `COMPOUND=yes`, size grows with realized gains but ignores losses that are still open. | Low | Size from account equity marked to market. |

## Mapping to the requested circuit breakers

| Requested rule | Current code | Where to enforce it |
|---|---|---|
| Max drawdown kill-switch: daily X%, monthly Y% | Missing | Start of `cycle()` (line 347). It must flatten positions (using the `panic()` path at 388-395) and keep `locked_until` in state, not in memory. |
| Per-trade risk cap Z% of equity | Missing, and there is no stop distance to measure (#1) | Inside `place_orders` before `new_order` (lines 321-342): reject when `qty*(entry−stop) > Z%·equity`. |
| Tail-risk / volatility filter (spread, ATR, latency over N ms) | Only the 35% crash guard at entry (lines 186-191) | Inside `triggers()` / `place_orders`, before any entry. |
| Consecutive-loss cooldown (N losses, T hours) | Per-coin cooldown only (line 127) | `record()` (lines 120-137) counts consecutive losses; `place_orders` checks the pause. It also needs #7 fixed, otherwise wipe-outs are not counted as losses. |

**Caveat:** all reproductions run on the repo's fake exchange, not real Binance, and scenarios A–G use made-up price paths. The −54%, −43%, −22%, 99–100%, −61%/−66% figures come from the research files cited above, not from new backtests run by this agent.
