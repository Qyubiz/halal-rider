# Round m16: predicting the size of the risk and betting less on risky buys

One Opus contestant (`m16_riskmodel_*.py`, logs `results_m16/`); pass rule `M14_PREREG.md`. Walk-forward LightGBM
(monthly retrain, expanding window, only outcomes known before the month) predicts each 90% mode buy's downside
(worst close within 96h, or a loss > 10%) from the 39 m10 features, with or without 10 futures features
(funding/premium from 2020, open interest from Dec 2021). Buys with a high score get a smaller bet (floor 25-50%);
nothing is skipped, so the trades and win rate are exactly 90% mode's.

Result: no pick (0 of 60 designs pass the 2021-23 bars; the two that pass bars 1-2 fail the neighbour bar).

- Skill on the trades 90% mode buys: rank correlation with the actual worst close about +0.25, steady across years
  (the model does see how deep a dip may go); AUC for "loss > 10%" 0.3-0.9 and unstable (0-9 such losses a year).
- Fallback (q 0.9, floor 0.5, not a pick): 2024-26 92.6 / +6.90 / -26.7 (90% mode 92.6 / +6.89 / -35.4);
  2018-20 92.5 / +10.95 / -52.8 (92.5 / +10.82 / -66.3); worst trade in 2024-26 -13.7% vs -27.3%;
  stressed 2024-26 +5.36 vs +5.49 (item 4 fails). Luck check: edge -0.07 pts/month (p 0.51) in 2024-26 and
  -0.34 (p 0.58) in 2018-20, rerun by the manager.
- Reading: smaller bets on high-risk buys cut the worst drop a lot at about the same profit; they do not add profit.
No bot code or settings change.
