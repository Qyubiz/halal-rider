# Round m22: two fixes that make the live bot match the backtest, checked by a full replay

`m22_replay_bnb.py` replays the real `panic_buyer.py` hour by hour through the fake exchange in 90% mode,
2024-01-01 to 2026-10-22, on the 18 coins with complete data (`results_m22/replay_bnb.log`).
Engine on the same 18 coins with the bot's timing: 144 trades, 91.7% wins, +7.22%/month (+7.45% at a 0.075% fee).

## 1. One coin at a time (`max_positions`, set to 1 in the 90% and 10% mode files)
`n_orders` limits new bids per hour, not coins held. When the account holds exactly the bot's capital (as the paper
bots do), the main order uses 98% of the cash and the bot then placed a second, small buy with the rest: 232 trades
where the backtest has 144, on a different path. With `max_positions: 1` the real code makes 145 trades at 91.7%
wins and +7.30%/month against the engine's 144, 91.7% and +7.22%. The uncapped run ended higher here, but that is
one path of a design whose result swings with timing (see `M18.md`); every published number is for one coin at a
time. Default (0 = no limit) is unchanged; the combined-mode file is unchanged (its replay was validated as is).

10% mode with the cap (`results_m22/replay_10pct.log`): real bot 113 trades, 77.0% wins, +8.00%/month; engine on
the same coins 112 trades, 76.8%, +7.92%.

## 2. Fees paid with BNB (`BNB_FEES=yes`)
With the switch the bot reads each buy's real fee from the order reply or Binance's trade list and sells exactly
what it bought. Same 145 trades; +0.24 points a month over normal fees (the engine says +0.23). Without the switch
the bot assumes the fee was taken in the coin and leaves 0.1% of every buy unsold: 550 USDT of loose coins by the
end of the run. If BNB runs out or the trade list cannot be read, the bot falls back to the normal-fee assumption and
never sells more than it bought (`tests/test_fees.py`). Off by default.
