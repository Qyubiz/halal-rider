# Round m11: five ways to combine the m10 pieces into one bot

Goal: one single-account bot that beats 90% mode on BOTH win rate and %/month out of sample. Same rules as m10
(`M10.md`): Shariah spot only, fully funded, 0.1% fee per side, 0.1% trade-through fills, no lookahead; each
combination picked on 2021-23 by a rule written before the run (win rate >= 93.8%, worst drop no deeper than -64%,
also with every fill 0.2% worse, best %/month averaged over nearby timing versions), then one look at 2024-26,
since Jan 2025 and the 2018-20 holdout. Every engine first reproduced 90% mode exactly with its new parts off.
Scripts: `m11_<angle>_*.py`; logs: `results_m11/<angle>/`.

## Fusion, checked again (`m11_verify_fusion.py`, `results_m11/fusion/verify.log`, hindsight)
With the 35% guard of 90% mode the pick gives 2024-26 92.7 / +8.4, since 2025 93.5 / +7.8, 2018-20 89.5 / +9.8
(fills 0.2% worse: 88.1 / +4.7 / -78). Over 81 neighbours (6h high +-2h, 9-11% dip, +1.5-2.5%, calm 6-8%):
- beat 90% mode on both win rate and %/month: 46% in 2024-26, 42% since 2025, 4% on the 2018-20 holdout,
  1% on the holdout with worse fills;
- 38 of 81 lose 95% or more in the stressed 2021-23 run (LUNA), even with the 35% guard: the fast second trigger
  buys collapsing coins.

## Why combining does not help
- All the pieces are dip-buyers on the same coins: they buy in the same crashes, so a second engine's cash is tied up
  in falling positions exactly when the core's best trades arrive (cashuse, stack).
- The extra profit of every combination comes from 2021 (sharp dips every week); after 2023 such dips are rare.
- Filters that predict losers in 2021-23 do not predict them later (confluence: only the 7-day volatility vote held
  in both test periods, and it trades win rate for profit).
- Switching between strategies by past results switches too late (allocator).
