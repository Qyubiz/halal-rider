# Round m20: smaller losing months at the same profit (bet less on risky-looking buys)

Rule written first: `M20_PREREG.md`. Scripts `m20_flags.py` (select / final / menu), `m20_surrogate.py`; logs
`results_m20/`. 90% mode's trades are untouched; a flagged buy uses 50% or 33% of the money.

## Result: no tool keeps the profit and removes the losing months
- Selection (2021-23, 48 designs, 5 nearby versions): flags that keep the profit within 0.25 points/month do not cut
  the losing months; flags that cut them by 40-55% cost about 1 point/month. The registered pick ("last close 2%+
  under the bid -> bet 50%") fails items 1-4 on the test periods (`results_m20/final_pick.log`). No tool added.
- Hindsight description of the trade-off (`results_m20/menu_hindsight.log`), profit kept / month-loss cut /
  worst month:

| Tool | 2021-23 | 2024-26 | 2018-20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90% mode | +13.17%/mo / - / -30.2% | +6.89%/mo / - / -11.8% | +10.82%/mo / - / -42.5% |
| every buy at 50% | 55% / 57% / -14.5% | 51% / 56% / -5.9% | 54% / 55% / -19.9% |
| coin >15% under its 7-day high -> 50% | 97% / 47% / -26.5% | 79% / 32% / -6.0% | 80% / 34% / -32.8% |
| m16 model, riskiest 10% -> 50% | 101% / 22% / -31.0% | 100% / 27% / -11.8% | 101% / 16% / -25.4% |
| m16 model, riskiest 20% -> 50% | 95% / 50% / -20.8% | 93% / 25% / -11.8% | 96% / 19% / -25.4% |

  The model flag keeps the profit with normal fills but loses about 1 point/month in the stressed 2021-23 run and
  cannot be reduced to simple rules (a depth-3 tree fitted to its flag is right about 14% of the time,
  `results_m20/surrogate.log`). The 7-day-high flag is about as good as simply betting 75% on every buy.
  Flagged-vs-random luck checks: p 0.04-0.4 per period, nothing proven.

## The -11.8% month (June 2026) is one trade
BCH bought 3 June 2026 03:00 after a 14% dip, no bounce in 96h, sold 7 June at -11.8%; no other trade that month.
At the buy it was 24% under its 7-day high. The m16 model scored it as low risk (0.009; flag level 0.051).
The ">15% under the 7-day high" flag does catch it, but it also flags 72 of the 148 trades since 2024, of which 67
were winners: halving those costs about a fifth of the profit.
