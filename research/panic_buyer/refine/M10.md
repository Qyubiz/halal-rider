# Round m10: ten parallel searches for a higher win rate and more profit per month

Goal: beat 90% mode (1 coin x 100%, 14% under the 24h-high close, +2.5%, 96h, 3h cooldown, crash guard 35%) on
win rate and %/month. Rules for every search: Shariah spot only, fully funded orders, 0.1% fee per side,
slippage, limit fills only when price trades 0.1% through, no lookahead. Settings chosen on 2021-23 only by a rule
written before the run, then one look at 2024-26, since Jan 2025 and the untouched 2018-20 holdout, normal fills
and every fill 0.2% worse (scalp and meanrev also tested 0.1% worse). Each search first reproduced 90% mode
exactly through its own engine. Scripts: `m10_<angle>_*.py`; logs: `results_m10/<angle>/`.

## Velocity, checked again (`m10_verify_velocity.py`, `results_m10/velocity/verify.log`)
The only pick with more %/month in every period. Split into its two parts and 27 + 12 neighbours:
- Re-arming a coin right after a win alone: better from 2021 (2024-26 92.9 / +7.7, stressed +5.9 vs +5.5) but
  worse on the holdout (+8.0 vs +10.8; +4.6 vs +7.4 with worse fills).
- The 12h trigger alone: better on the holdout (+12.7) but win rate 90.8% and stressed 2024-26 +5.0 vs +5.5.
- Neighbours of the 12h trigger (8/12/16h high, 12-13% dip, +2.5-3.5%): win rate below 90% mode in every
  out-of-sample period; with worse fills or stress most make no more than 90% mode. A 14% second trigger is
  identical to the first (a 12h high is never above the 24h high).

## What the ten searches say about "win every trade"
- Tiny targets lose: +0.5% take-profits average -0.36% to +0.10% per trade at 94-99% wins, because 0.2% of fees
  per round trip and the rare large loss outweigh many tiny wins.
- 97-99% win rates exist only with ways of not taking losses (averaging down, holding losers for weeks); those
  designs had -55% to -95% drops and about 0%/month after 2023.
- Losing trades look like winners when the bot buys: neither 755 regime filters nor a machine-learning model could
  tell them apart out of sample.
- Opportunities shrank after 2023 for every dip-based design (sharp one-hour and one-day drops are rarer).
